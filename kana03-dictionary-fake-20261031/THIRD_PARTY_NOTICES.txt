=============================================

Transform: convert hiragana readings into katakana, deduplicate identical
readings for each spelling, favor the upstream optimized subset when present,
retain up to eight distinct readings per given-name spelling, and split
the 70,296 given-name spellings into eight JS files for offline loading.
No patient record or clinical data was used to create these dictionary files.

This dataset's names may have several valid pronunciations. Software-produced
readings are suggestions ONLY; verify each reading before confirming/saving.
This dataset does NOT certify the true reading for any actual person.
